Meet Your FlowTherapist

Care for the whole person.

Massage is healthcare—not a luxury.

Hi, I’m Brandy Hennigan, LMT—a trauma-informed therapeutic massage therapist, lifelong student of human movement, and firm believer in the healing power of touch.

I work with people experiencing persistent tension, stress, headaches, movement restrictions, recovery needs, and the everyday wear and tear life places on the body.

I believe care should be collaborative. You’re encouraged to ask questions, communicate what you need, and move at a pace that feels right for you. Every session is tailored to your goals because no two bodies—or wellness journeys—are the same.

My goal is to help you move with greater ease, build resilience, and feel more at home in your body.
